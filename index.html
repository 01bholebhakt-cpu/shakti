Who we are

A foundation built around people.

Shakti Sparsh Foundation is a community-focused social initiative based in Katihar, Bihar. Our purpose is simple: identify genuine needs, mobilize support and create pathways towards a more dignified life.

Our work can span immediate relief and long-term empowerment — from distributing food and clothing to supporting education, healthcare, children, senior citizens, families and communities during difficult times.

“Sparsh Insaniyat Ka, Sankalp Badlav Ka.”
Food SecurityEducationHealthcareChild WelfareElderly CareEmergency Relief

From immediate relief to lasting opportunity.

We want every initiative to do more than solve a momentary problem — it should create confidence, dignity and a reason to hope.
